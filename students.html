Student Association

Computer Engineering Students' Association (COESA) Executives

President

Leads the student association, represents student interests to department leadership, and coordinates society events.

Vice President

Supports the President and oversees academic affairs, including tutorial sessions and study groups.

Secretary General

Manages association records, meeting minutes, and communications with members.

Financial Secretary

Manages association dues, budgeting, and event funding.

Welfare Officer

Coordinates student welfare initiatives and peer support programs.

Public Relations Officer

Manages association social media and external communications.

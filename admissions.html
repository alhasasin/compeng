Admissions

Undergraduate

UTME Admission Requirements

O'Level Requirements

Five credits at O'Level (WAEC/NECO), including English Language, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and one other science subject, obtained at not more than two sittings.

UTME Subject Combination & Minimum Score

Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and English Language. ABU's general minimum UTME score for Post-UTME eligibility is 180, though Computer Engineering (like other Engineering courses) is competitive and typically requires a higher aggregate — confirm the current departmental cut-off on the official ABU admissions portal each session.

Post-UTME Screening & Aggregate

Candidates who choose ABU as first choice and meet the minimum UTME score sit ABU's Post-UTME screening. Admission is based on an aggregate combining JAMB and Post-UTME scores (JAMB score ÷ 8, plus Post-UTME score ÷ 2), not JAMB score alone.

Direct Entry

Candidates with a relevant ND/HND, or A-Level passes in Mathematics and Physics, may apply for direct entry into 200 level.

Postgraduate

PGD / M.Sc. / Ph.D. Requirements

PGD / M.Sc. Requirements

A B.Eng./B.Sc. in Computer Engineering or a closely related field, meeting the minimum CGPA set by the School of Postgraduate Studies, plus any required departmental interview.

Ph.D. Requirements

A relevant M.Sc. with a minimum CGPA as specified by the School of Postgraduate Studies, along with a research proposal and interview.

Process

How to Apply

1. Register for UTME

Register with JAMB and select Ahmadu Bello University and Computer Engineering as your first choice.

2. Sit Post-UTME

If shortlisted, register for and sit ABU's Post-UTME screening test.

3. Check Admission Status

Monitor your admission status on the JAMB CAPS portal and the ABU admissions portal.

4. Accept & Register

Accept your admission offer, pay acceptance fees, and complete registration at the start of the session.

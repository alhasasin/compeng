Academics

Academic Programs

Undergraduate and postgraduate degrees designed to build both theoretical depth and practical engineering skill.

Undergraduate

B.Eng. Computer Engineering

Program Overview

A five-year full-time program covering digital systems, computer architecture, embedded systems, software engineering, networking, and control systems, with a final-year research project.

Duration & Structure

5 years (10 semesters), including a mandatory Students Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES) placement in the penultimate year.

Sample Course Outline

Sample undergraduate course outline by level
LevelCore Courses
100Engineering Mathematics, Introduction to Computing, Physics for Engineers
200Digital Logic Design, Data Structures, Circuit Theory
300Computer Architecture, Microprocessor Systems, Operating Systems, SIWES
400Embedded Systems, Computer Networks, Software Engineering
500Final Year Project, Control Systems, Engineering Management

Postgraduate

PGD, M.Sc. and Ph.D. Programs

Postgraduate Diploma (PGD) Computer Engineering

A conversion-level program for graduates from related disciplines seeking to build computer engineering foundations.

M.Sc. Computer Engineering

Research-oriented master's program building on undergraduate computer engineering training.

Ph.D. Computer Engineering

Doctoral research program for candidates pursuing advanced, original contributions to the field.

Areas of Specialization

Embedded Systems

Microcontrollers, IoT, and real-time systems design.

Computer Networks

Network infrastructure, protocols, and security.

Control Engineering

Application design, development methodologies, and testing.

Artificial Intelligence

Machine learning, data science, and intelligent systems.

About Us

About the Department

A history of engineering excellence at the heart of Nigeria's academic community.

Our History

Engineering education at Ahmadu Bello University traces back to 1955, when it began as a Faculty of Engineering of the University College, Ibadan, based at the Nigerian College of Arts, Science and Technology (N.C.A.S.T.) in Zaria, offering courses leading to external London University degrees in Civil, Electrical, and Electrical & Computer Engineering. Following the 1961 Ashby Commission recommendations, Ahmadu Bello University was opened in October 1962, and the Faculty of Engineering — and in time the Department of Computer Engineering within it — was born under the University's own degree programs.

Our Objectives

In line with the University's founding objectives under Article 4 of its 1962 and 1975 Laws, the Department exists to provide regular, updated programs of the highest academic standard; to equip students with the technical knowledge, skills, and competencies to analyze, design, develop, test, and produce computer engineering products and services for the benefit of humanity; to promote research and advancement in the field; and to secure the diffusion of computer engineering knowledge throughout Nigeria and beyond.

Our Vision

To be a leading center of excellence in computer engineering education and research in Africa, recognized for producing innovative engineers and impactful research that addresses real-world challenges.

Accreditation

Our undergraduate and postgraduate programs are accredited by the National Universities Commission (NUC) and recognized by the Council for the Regulation of Engineering in Nigeria (COREN), ensuring graduates meet national professional engineering standards.
